📋 FAMILY NUTRITION PROTOCOLS

ADULT NUTRITION TARGETS

  • Objective: Belly fat reduction
  • Morning detox waters with cumin/lemon
  • High fiber vegetables and millets
  • Controlled portions with red rice
  • Evening light meals
  • Minimal oil and processed foods

CHILD NUTRITION MATRIX (2.6Y)

  • Critical: Cow milk morning + night
  • No spicy food, minimal podi
  • Soft texture in all dishes
  • Boiled egg yolk 2-3 times/week
  • Mashed dal and vegetables
  • Encourage water, avoid juices/sweets
  • Ghee for healthy fat intake

⏰ MEAL TIMING PROTOCOL

  • Morning: 7:00-8:00 AM
  • Mid-morning: Soaked almonds + date (adults)
  • Lunch: 12:00-1:00 PM
  • Evening snack: Buttermilk/fruit (4:00 PM)
  • Dinner: 7:00-8:00 PM
  • Bedtime: Herbal water (adults only)

💡 COST OPTIMIZATION STRATEGIES

  • Buy vegetables twice weekly
  • Reuse chutneys and curries smartly
  • Make curd at home
  • Batch cook grains and dals
  • Use seasonal vegetables
  • Minimal food waste policy

🚨 HEALTH ALERTS

  • De-seed tomatoes (kidney stone prevention)
  • Limit spinach/beetroot (oxalates)
  • Fresh curry leaves preferred
  • Cold-pressed oils when possible
  • Avoid bulk buying perishables

⚡ EXECUTION STRATEGIES

🚀 BATCH COOKING PLAN

  • Sunday: Make large batch of idli/dosa batter
  • Monday: Extra sambar for multiple meals
  • Wednesday: Cook extra rice for coming days
  • Sunday: Prepare chicken curry in bulk
  • Daily: Make extra chutneys for next day

🆘 BACKUP MEAL OPTIONS

  • Quick Breakfasts: Poha, bread toast, upma mix
  • Emergency Lunches: Curd rice + pickle, lemon rice
  • Simple Dinners: Ready dosa batter, chapati + dal
  • Protein Boosters: Boiled eggs, paneer cubes
  • Child Meals: Banana + milk, mashed khichdi

⏱️ TIME-SAVING HACKS

  • Pre-cut vegetables twice weekly
  • Use pressure cooker for simultaneous cooking
  • Keep pre-made spice mixes ready
  • Frozen grated coconut for chutneys
  • Soak dals/grains overnight when possible
